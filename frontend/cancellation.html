Cancellation & Refund Policy

Sample Policy Notice: This is a placeholder cancellation policy for demonstration purposes. Actual businesses must define specific cancellation terms, refund percentages, and timelines appropriate to their operations and legal requirements.

1. Cancellation by Customer

You may cancel your booking at any time by contacting us or through your booking management page. Cancellation charges depend on how far in advance you cancel before your trip start date.

Sample Cancellation Timeline

Cancellation Period Cancellation Charge Refund Amount
30+ days before departure 10% of total booking value 90% refund
15-29 days before departure 25% of total booking value 75% refund
8-14 days before departure 50% of total booking value 50% refund
3-7 days before departure 75% of total booking value 25% refund
Less than 3 days or no-show 100% of total booking value No refund

Note: The cancellation period is calculated from the date of cancellation request to the trip start date. These are sample percentages and actual policy should be defined based on business requirements.

2. How to Cancel

To cancel your booking:

  1. Visit the My Bookings page
  2. Locate your booking using your reference number
  3. Click "Request Cancellation"
  4. Confirm the cancellation request

3. Refund Processing

Once your cancellation is processed:

  • Refund amounts are calculated based on the applicable cancellation charges
  • Refunds are initiated within 7-10 business days of cancellation approval
  • Refunds are credited to the original payment method
  • Bank processing may take an additional 5-7 business days
  • You will receive email confirmation when the refund is initiated

4. Cancellation by Marigold Routes

We reserve the right to cancel trips due to:

  • Insufficient participants (minimum group size not met)
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, political unrest, pandemics)
  • Safety concerns
  • Unforeseen circumstances beyond our control

In case of cancellation by Marigold Routes:

  • You will receive a full refund (100%) of the amount paid
  • Or you may choose to reschedule to alternative dates without additional charges
  • We will notify you as soon as possible, typically at least 7 days before departure

5. Modifications vs Cancellations

If you wish to modify your booking (change dates, upgrade hotels, add services) instead of canceling:

  • Contact us at least 15 days before departure
  • Modifications are subject to availability
  • Difference in package cost will be charged/refunded
  • A modification fee may apply (typically lower than cancellation charges)

6. Non-Refundable Components

Certain components may be non-refundable regardless of cancellation timing:

  • Special permits or entry fees already purchased
  • Third-party services with non-refundable terms
  • Customized arrangements made specifically for your booking

Any such components will be clearly communicated at the time of booking.

7. Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance that covers trip cancellation, medical emergencies, and other unforeseen events. Insurance may provide coverage even when our standard cancellation policy does not offer refunds.

Optional travel insurance is available during booking for ₹650 per person.

8. Force Majeure

In case of force majeure events (natural disasters, pandemics, government restrictions, etc.), standard cancellation charges may be waived or reduced at management discretion. We will work with customers to find the best possible solution, which may include:

  • Full or partial refunds
  • Rebooking to future dates without penalties
  • Credit vouchers valid for future bookings
