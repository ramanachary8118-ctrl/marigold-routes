About Marigold Routes

Guided trips across India made simple

Who we are

Marigold Routes is a sample travel and tourism platform showcasing a modern approach to booking guided tours across India. This website demonstrates a complete full-stack travel booking system.

Our platform features eight carefully curated destinations across India, from serene mountains and pristine coastlines to tranquil backwaters and historic heritage towns.

What we offer

Fixed pricing

See the complete price per person before you book. No hidden costs or surprise charges.

Ready-made packages

Choose from professionally planned itineraries sized for weekends or full holidays.

All-inclusive

Hotels, local transport, and a trip captain are included in every package.

Flexible dates

Start your trip any day from tomorrow onward. You choose when to travel.

How it works

  1. Browse destinations - Explore eight curated places across India
  2. Choose a package - Select from ready-made itineraries with fixed pricing
  3. Pick your dates - Choose any start date from tomorrow onward
  4. Book instantly - Complete your booking in just two minutes
  5. Travel with confidence - Hotels, transport, and trip captain included

Our packages include

✓ Accommodation in quality hotels
✓ Local transportation
✓ Experienced trip captain
✓ Day-by-day itinerary
✓ 24/7 support during travel
✓ GST and applicable taxes
