Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about booking and travelling with Marigold Routes

Booking

How do I book a trip?

Browse destinations or packages, select your preferred option, choose your travel dates, fill in traveller details, and confirm your booking. The entire process takes about two minutes.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets through our secure payment gateway powered by Razorpay.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All payment processing is handled by Razorpay, a certified PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not store any card details on our servers.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes, you can book for anyone. Simply enter the lead traveller's details when making the booking.

Pricing

What is included in the package price?

All packages include accommodation in standard hotels (twin sharing), local transportation, an experienced trip captain, and applicable taxes.

How is the child fare calculated?

Children aged 5 to 11 years are charged at 70% of the adult fare. Children under 5 travel free (not occupying a separate bed).

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The final price you see at checkout includes all applicable charges and GST. There are no hidden fees.

Can I upgrade my accommodation?

Yes, you can choose Superior or Luxury hotel categories when booking for an additional charge per night.

Travel dates and changes

Can I start my trip on any date?

Yes, you can choose any start date from tomorrow onward, subject to availability.

Can I modify my travel dates after booking?

Date changes may be possible subject to availability and our modification policy. Please contact us as soon as possible to request changes.

What if I need to cancel my trip?

Please refer to our Cancellation Policy for detailed information about cancellation timelines and applicable charges.

During the trip

What does a trip captain do?

Your trip captain is an experienced local guide who accompanies the group, manages logistics, provides insights about destinations, and ensures everything runs smoothly.

What meals are included?

Meal inclusions vary by package. Check your specific package itinerary for details. Typically, breakfast is included at all hotels.

What about travel insurance?

Travel insurance is optional but recommended. You can add it during booking for ₹650 per person.

How do I reach you during the trip?

You'll receive 24/7 support contact details before your trip. Your trip captain will also have direct communication with our support team.

Support

How can I view my booking?

Visit the My Bookings page. Bookings made on your device are automatically saved. You can also look up any booking using your reference number and email.

I didn't receive my booking confirmation email

Check your spam folder first. If you still can't find it, contact us with your booking reference number and we'll resend it.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach us by phone at 040 5550 0148 (Mon-Fri, 9 AM - 6 PM IST) or email us at hello@marigoldroutes.example. You can also use our contact form.

Still have questions?

We're here to help. Get in touch with our team.
